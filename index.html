So… what happened to Digital Hustle Africa?

The reunion. Where we thought our careers would go, where they actually went, and whether we still believe in the word “hustle”.

Dropping soon

Let’s save you some time.

It is

  • Real talk about work, money, family and home.
  • Practical sometimes. Personal sometimes. Funny when it can’t be helped.
  • Two hosts who disagree out loud and stay friends.
  • For anyone figuring out what their life looks like across a border.

It isn’t

  • A tech podcast. (Fine, tech comes up.)
  • A “make money online in 7 days” show.
  • Another reel of polished success stories.
  • Two people explaining Africa to you from a podium.

Who’s this for?

Chances are, you’re one of these.

  • Building a career at home
  • Thinking about leaving
  • Freshly landed, still finding the shops
  • Settled abroad, still “from” somewhere
  • Raising kids far from the grandparents
  • Quietly planning the trip back

Not African but curious? Pull up a chair, you’re welcome too.
