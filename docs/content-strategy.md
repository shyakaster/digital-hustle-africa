# Digital Hustle Africa — Website Content Strategy

Audience-facing only. Nothing here describes how the show is run behind the scenes.

## The one-line idea

Honest conversations about how Africans, at home and abroad, are actually building lives, careers and ideas when
geography no longer decides everything.

## Positioning

|         | Is                                                  | Isn't                                |
| ------- | --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Format  | Two friends and a guest, talking                    | A panel of experts                   |
| Tone    | Practical, personal, funny, sometimes uncomfortable | Motivational, polished, performed    |
| Subject | Work, migration, belonging, reinvention, going home | A "tech" or "make money online" show |
| Stance  | Hosts are in the same questions as the listener     | Explaining Africa to an audience     |

**Voice:** a smart friend at the dinner table. Cheeky in the headline, serious in the substance. Short sentences. No
hustle-culture clichés unless we are teasing them. Never "unlock", "journey", "empower", "game-changer".

**The "hustle" word:** the site keeps it and winks at it: hustle as honest effort, not grind-porn.

## Audience

Any African adult navigating ambition, work and change. Six doorways, each used as a chip on the home page and a
topic tag on episodes:

1. Building a career at home
2. Thinking about leaving
3. Freshly landed somewhere new
4. Established abroad
5. Raising kids far from extended family
6. Thinking about going back

Secondary: non-African listeners curious about these questions.

## Site map

| Page     | Job                                                             | Primary action  |
| -------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------- |
| Home     | Say what this is in 10 seconds; show the season; find your door | Listen / follow |
| Episodes | Browse all eight conversations; each one leads with a question  | Play or follow  |

Deliberately not built yet: blog, community, events, mentorship, opportunity matching, merch. Add them only after
listeners ask.

## Page-level copy rules

- **Home hero:** a claim, not a description. Then one sentence of what the show is, then the listen buttons.
- **Every episode** has: number, title, one-line hook, a "Ask yourself" question for the listener, topic tags.
- **Honesty about state:** until an episode is live, the card says "Coming soon". No fake players, play counts or
  testimonials.
- **Hosts** are described by where they are and what they are wrestling with, not by credentials.

## Per-episode content kit (website side)

One conversation feeds every page element: title, hook, a pull-quote, 3 timestamps, the "Ask yourself" question,
and a short written reflection. Shorter derivatives (clips, carousels, LinkedIn posts) point back to the episode
page.

## What listeners should do

1. Press play (or follow on a platform).
2. Send the episode to the one person it reminds them of.
3. Tell us if "this felt like my life" — the strongest signal we can receive.

## Open items for the team

- A contact address or form for guest suggestions and listener stories.
- Host photos.
