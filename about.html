About the show

What happens to a life when geography stops deciding?

Digital Hustle Africa began in 2019, from conversations at Andela about a very specific moment: the idea that where you lived mattered less, and that remote work and digital careers were opening doors that hadn’t existed before.

Then life happened. The questions didn’t go away. If anything they got more interesting. So we’re back, asking something bigger: how are Africans, at home and across the diaspora, actually building lives, careers and ideas right now?

Not a tech podcast. Not a “make money online” show. Not another reel of polished success stories.

Just honest conversation. Practical, personal, funny and, now and then, uncomfortable.

How we show up

  • Stories over titles

    A good story beats an impressive job title every time.

  • Disagreement welcome

    We’ve lived different versions of this. We say so, politely, and then not so politely.

  • Gains and costs

    Opportunity is real. So is the bill. We talk about both.
